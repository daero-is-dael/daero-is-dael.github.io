Privacy Policy

Dael Assistant · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Dael Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner, Dael Lee, with his own Google account. This policy explains what Google data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, the app may access:

  • Google Calendar: events, to view and manage the owner's schedule
  • Gmail: messages, to read, organize, draft and send email on the owner's behalf
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: files, to read, create and edit the owner's documents
  • Google Contacts: read-only, to look up the owner's contacts

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to those tasks.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except that content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner uses to carry out his own requests.
  • No data from other users is collected, because the app has no other users.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dael Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading any further data.
