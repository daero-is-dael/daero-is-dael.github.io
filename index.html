Dael Assistant

A personal productivity assistant used only by its owner, Dael Lee.

What it does

Dael Assistant is a private tool that helps its owner manage his own work. With the owner's permission it reads and organizes his own Google Calendar events, Gmail messages, Drive files, Docs, Sheets and contacts, and drafts or sends messages on his behalf.

Who can use it

This app is not offered to the public. It is used only with the owner's own Google account.
